### ASR footprint (2026-10-26, estimates from model specs, not measured)

Rules of thumb: fp16 = 2 bytes/param, fp32 = 4, int8 = 1; plus ~0.3-0.5 GB CUDA context and ~1 GB of Python/PyTorch libraries in RAM. Whisper models always encode a padded 30 s window, so short clips cost as much as long ones; FastConformer cost scales with clip length. CPU times are rough guesses for a 4-core laptop CPU and a ~5 s segment.

| Model | Params | Weights fp16 / int8 | Est. VRAM | Est. RAM (CPU-only) | Est. CPU time per 5 s segment | Measured GPU s/clip |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nvidia FastConformer | 115M | 0.23 GB / - (fp32 0.46 GB) | ~0.6-1 GB | ~1.5-2 GB | ~0.3-0.8 s | 0.08 |
| whisper-large-v3-turbo (faster-whisper) | 809M | 1.6 GB / 0.8 GB | ~2-2.5 GB | ~1.5-2 GB (int8) | ~2-5 s | 0.70 |
| Chillarmo turbo (transformers) | 809M | 1.6 GB / - (fp32 3.2 GB) | ~2-2.5 GB | ~4 GB (fp32) | ~4-10 s (int8 after CTranslate2 conversion: like turbo) | 0.80 |
| BuzzASR (large-v3) | 1.55B | 3.1 GB / 1.6 GB | ~3.5-4 GB | ~7 GB (fp32) | ~10-20 s | not run |
| Seamless M4T v2 large | 2.3B total (speech-to-text path roughly 1.5B) | ~3-4.6 GB | ~4-5 GB | ~7-10 GB (fp32) | ~5-15 s | 0.78 |

The pipeline also needs NLLB-200 600M for translation: ~1.2 GB fp16 VRAM, ~0.5-1.5 s per sentence on CPU.

Notes on the first live run (2026-10-24):
- `capture` is a steady 0.25 s: Silero needs 300 ms of silence (`min_silence_duration_ms=300`) to close a segment, minus the 100 ms speech pad.
- WER is a word-level score after lowercasing and removing punctuation. Most errors are spelling-level (սուրջ for սուրճ, առավության for առավոտյան) or wrong word splits (Իմանունը, մայրակ հաղաքն), so CER is much lower than WER.
- Translation was right in meaning for 3 of 10 (lines 5, 9, roughly 3). Wrong ASR spellings produce confident wrong English (սուրջ -> "seriously", Արամ heard as առամ -> "dragon").
- Line 10 (Շնորհակալություն, ցտեսություն) came out as Tibetan-script garbage: a hallucination with avg_logprob -0.80. It is printed and visible, as the rules require.
